Validator Considerations

  1. Primary matching: First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth. Barangay/Purok may be added as a tie-breaker to reduce false matches.
  2. Staff override: If a duplicate is detected but the resident insists they have no account, Barangay Staff can verify identity, correct encoding errors, or approve a new registration if the records do not actually belong to the same person.
